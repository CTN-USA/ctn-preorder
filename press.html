DALLAS and JACKSONVILLE, Fla. — Nov. 2, 2026, 8:00 a.m. ET

CTN USA, the U.S. arm of Finnish wellness-technology company Cryotech Nordic (CTN), today announced the global premiere of the OxyPro™ VH2, a modular, two-seat mild hyperbaric chamber with an integrated oxygen generator and a lounge-style interior. CTN will unveil the OxyPro VH2 at a press conference at 7:40 a.m. CT on Thursday, November 12, before the show floor opens at Beyond Activ Americas 2026, at CTN Booth #401 in the Trinity Exhibition Hall at the Hilton Anatole in Dallas. Pre-orders open today, exclusively through CTN, and units will be delivered on a first-come, first-delivered basis.

Hyperbaric sessions don’t have to be a solo experience. The OxyPro VH2 brings a two-seat layout to the chamber experience: a private, lounge-style interior where two people recline side by side so teams, fitness clubs, spas and recovery studios can double session capacity and offer a premium lounge-style experience. CTN is a Platinum Partner of Beyond Activ Americas, which takes place November 12–13, 2026.

What sets the OxyPro VH2 apart

  • Two seats, two electric recliners — double capacity vs. single-person chambers.
  • On-board oxygen generator — 93% ± 3% oxygen at 20 L/min; no external tanks to store or swap.
  • Adjustable pressure — up to 1.5 ATA within the manufacturer’s operating range.
  • Dual touchscreen control — inside and outside, with intercom.
  • Pressure-control features — pressure-grade steel, dual automatic relief and dual manual emergency valves.
  • Lounge-grade interior — eco-leather, hardwood flooring, dual-color LED, display and climate control.
  • Compact footprint — about 2.2 × 1.8 m; companion compressor draws 3,300 W.
  • Engineered in Finland, supported in the U.S. — factory-direct service from Jacksonville, FL.
