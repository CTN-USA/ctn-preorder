Questions

Before you reserve.

What does pre-ordering involve?

You complete a short form with your contact, company and delivery details. We email a written quote with a one-click accept link. After you accept, we send payment instructions (or start Brickhouse financing). It takes about two minutes to start.

How is my place in line decided?

Production, shipping and installation follow the order in which pre-orders are confirmed. A pre-order is confirmed when your 50% deposit is received or your Brickhouse financing is approved.

How much does it cost?

Your emailed quote shows pricing for your configuration, delivery and installation. There's no obligation until you accept it.

Can CTN-USA install it?

Yes. CTN-USA can arrange professional installation, or your own team or contractor can handle it. You choose on the form.

What does my site need?

Space for the chamber (about 2.2 m wide × 1.9 m deep × 2.0 m tall, or 7.2 × 6.1 × 6.6 ft) and its companion units, clear access for delivery, and dedicated electrical supply prepared by your electrician. Exact requirements come with your quote. The form collects the details we need to plan delivery with you.

What support is included?

CTN-USA provides factory-direct service, parts and support from Jacksonville, Florida, backed by the manufacturer's warranty, with maintenance plans available.

Is there a smaller option?

Yes. The OxyPro VH1 is also available to pre-order. Choose it on the pre-order form, or call us and we will help you pick the right fit for your space.

Can I order more than one?

Yes. Choose your quantity of VH1 or VH2 units on the form. Multi-unit orders for clubs, teams, hotels and groups are welcome.
