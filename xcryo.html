How it works

Focused cold air, under automated control.

X°CRYO sends a high-flow stream of cold air across the target area, rapidly lowering surface and near-surface tissue temperature. Airflow is adjustable across three power levels for client comfort.

  • Short sessions, typically a few minutes per area
  • No ice, no mess, no downtime for the client
  • Touchscreen programs with automated timing
  • Simple to learn, with setup and staff training available from CTN USA
