The two models

OxyPro VH1 and VH2.

Stainless-steel mild hyperbaric chambers at 1.5 ATA. VH1 seats 1 person. VH2 seats 2 people, side by side. Power: 220V, requires two dedicated outlets. Your electrician prepares that supply.

OxyPro VH1

One-person stainless hardshell

  • Capacity: 1 person
  • Pressure: 1.5 ATA
  • Material: Stainless steel
  • Chamber size: L 1820 × W 1200 × H 1753 mm (~71.7 × 47.2 × 69.0 in)
  • Chamber weight: 380 kg (~838 lb)
  • Power: 220V, requires two dedicated outlets
  • Oxygen flow: 20 L/min via external concentrators
  • Intercom; internal & external controls

OxyPro VH2

Two-person stainless hardshell · Beyond Activ world premiere

  • Capacity: 2 people, side by side
  • Pressure: 1.5 ATA
  • Material: Stainless steel
  • Chamber size: 2,204 × 1,867 × 2,025 mm (W × D × H)
  • Power: 220V, requires two dedicated outlets
  • Oxygen flow: 20 L/min via external concentrators
  • Climate-controlled interior; dual controls
